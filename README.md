Neon Heist Canvas — a GTA 6-style cover art maker with eight polygon layouts (solo, diagonal duo, hero slant, offset strips, staggered quad, triptych, a radial burst with a sun-medallion hub, and a new chevron band), per-panel colour grades, photo fit modes and anchors that keep faces in frame, a code-drawn Vice City sunset backdrop (gradient sky, clouds, striped sun, a bay with a sun reflection, skyline and palm silhouettes), multi-zone typography (title with a neon echo, glow and outline, subtitle ribbon, tagline on a dark pill, stamp badge that follows the title), film grain, halftone and optional scanlines, and PNG export at 1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1080×1920 and 1920×1080 — plus one-click batch export and copy-to-clipboard. Runs fully offline in a desktop browser; your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080, load one or more photos (or use the three bundled samples), pick a layout, grade and anchor each panel, write your title, subtitle and tagline, and export the PNG. One click on “Surprise me” builds a whole random poster.
