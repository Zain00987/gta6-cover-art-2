Neon Heist Canvas — a GTA 6-style cover art maker with seven polygon layouts (solo, diagonal duo, hero slant, offset strips, staggered quad, triptych, and a new radial burst with a hexagon hub), per-panel colour grades and photo fit modes (fill or whole photo), a code-drawn Vice City sunset backdrop (gradient sky, striped sun, palm silhouettes, noir stars), multi-zone typography (title with neon glow and outline, subtitle ribbon, tagline on a dark pill, stamp badge that follows the title), film grain and halftone, and PNG export at 1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1080×1920 and 1920×1080. Runs fully offline in a desktop browser; your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080, load one or more photos (or use the two bundled samples), pick a layout, grade and fit each panel, write your title, subtitle and tagline, and export the PNG. One click on “Surprise me” builds a whole random poster.
